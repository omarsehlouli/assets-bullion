BULLION TRADING LLC — CAMPAIGN KNOWLEDGE BRIEF
Research date: 12 October 2026. Source snapshots are dated in source_index.json.

BUSINESS AND PURCHASE PATH
The public site presents Bullion Trading LLC as a physical precious-metals dealer serving customers since 2008, with a Diamond District counter and U.S. online sales. It sells gold and silver bars, coins and rounds, plus platinum, palladium, casting grain and gift products. The campaign opportunity is the combination of recognizable products, current product pricing, educational material and a reachable local business.
Sources: https://bulliontradingllc.com/ ; https://bulliontradingllc.com/about-us/ ; https://bulliontradingllc.com/contact-us/

The address is 20 West 47th Street, Lower Level #24, New York, NY 10036. Public telephone: +1 646-362-3536. The location page lists Monday–Thursday 8:30 a.m.–4 p.m. and Friday 8:30 a.m.–1 p.m. Confirm current hours before a visit. The local ad should make it easy to move from product browsing to a conversation, directions and a purchase.
Source: https://bulliontradingllc.com/location/

PRICING AND PURCHASE FRICTION
The site distinguishes current metal prices from finished-product prices and publishes quantity/payment tables. Premiums are not a single permanent percentage. Compare the same product, condition, weight, quantity and payment method at the same time. Shipping and applicable taxes can affect the total. Campaign2 turns this into a concrete purchase task: compare 1 oz bars and current payment options.
Source: https://learn.bulliontradingllc.com/guides/questions/03-what-is-spot-price-vs-premium/

Recorded prices, chart values, inventory counts, example premium ranges and buyback amounts in the archive are historical observations, not current offers. A price shown in page headers can have a different update time from a product table. None is hard-coded into the five ads. The campaign set makes no market forecast, inflation-protection guarantee, return promise or lowest-price claim.

FULFILLMENT AND LOCAL PICKUP
Published terms describe U.S.-only shipping, payment verification and clearance requirements. They prohibit in-store collection for card and PayPal purchases. The terms describe insurance conditions and possible payment holds. Product-page shipping badges and the owner’s pickup transcript simplify timing differently. Campaign5 therefore asks buyers to contact the team and arrange eligible pickup, with clearance and payment conditions visible.
Source: https://bulliontradingllc.com/terms-and-conditions/ ; local transcript faq-pickup.srt

The terms also contain competing cancellation, return and payment-time statements. These need business clarification before creating any ad about easy returns, exact cancellation costs or guaranteed clearance times. The current five ads avoid those promises.

AUTHENTICITY AND TRUST
The site’s authentication guide says several checks are needed and acknowledges limits of single tests. The owner’s video describes in-office testing and inspection before shipping. Those operational statements are valuable source material, but should be confirmed before promising a specific test, device, certificate or test record to every customer. The current local trust concept uses the documented location and available conversation instead.
Source: https://learn.bulliontradingllc.com/guides/questions/24-how-to-tell-if-gold-or-silver-is-real/ ; local transcript faq-real-gold.srt

Dealer-selection content highlights transparent pricing, physical contact details, product recognition and questions about shipping and buyback. Those themes support first-purchase confidence. They do not establish independent verification of every company claim, review score, industry membership or advertised sales total.
Source: https://learn.bulliontradingllc.com/guides/questions/11-how-to-choose-reputable-precious-metals-dealer/

GIFTING AND EDUCATION
The traditions hub covers anniversaries, wedding coins, 21st birthdays, Lunar New Year, Dhanteras and Pidyon HaBen. These are useful editorial themes. Campaign3 uses an evergreen milestone so it can be judged without relying on a specific holiday date or an inferred religious identity. The gift box in the generated visual is styling only.
Sources: https://bulliontradingllc.com/traditions/ ; https://bulliontradingllc.com/gifts/

The learning center includes guides, questions, quizzes, price-history charts, product/metal comparisons, security topics and calculators. Educational destinations are useful secondary links. For this brief, primary ad links go to purchase-relevant categories or a matching product, so the user is not left in a long article when ready to shop.
Sources: https://learn.bulliontradingllc.com/guides/ ; https://learn.bulliontradingllc.com/tools/

BUYBACK CONTEXT
The sell-to-us page publishes a quote/price-lock flow, shipping instructions and inspection before payment. It can help explain the existence of a resale path, but it does not guarantee that a purchase can be resold at the original price or without a spread. These five campaigns pursue purchases, so seller-intent search terms are negatives.
Source: https://bulliontradingllc.com/sell-to-us/
