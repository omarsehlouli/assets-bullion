Collected 2026-10-12 for five purchase-focused Bullion Trading LLC campaigns.

Scope: the public business website, linked learning center, product pages, operational policies, and 15 local video transcripts in the parent project. Source product images are copied from the existing marketing project with their provenance manifests. This is a broad campaign research collection, not a claim to have captured every page or private business information.

User brief: U.S. buyers on Google and Meta, with some campaigns for New York local buyers. Primary outcome: purchases of physical gold and silver. Exactly five campaign concepts. No ads have been launched.

Evidence handling: website statements are first-party business claims. Transcripts are creative inputs, not independently verified operating policies. Quotes and prices can change. Conflicting payment, pickup, shipment, tax and authentication statements are flagged separately. No fixed metal prices, forecasts, guaranteed returns, invented reviews, or unverified savings figures are used in campaign copy.
