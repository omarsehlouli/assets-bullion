How much over spot should you really pay for gold?
There's no fixed premium. It can change throughout the day.
We constantly compare prices across the bullion market, and adjust for competition, inventory, and supply and demand.
The same item might be fifty dollars over spot in the morning, thirty a few hours later, and eighty or ninety later that day.
When a dealer has plenty of Gold Eagles or one-ounce bars, the premium may drop to move that inventory.
So it isn't a permanent markup. The market largely sets it.
To judge a premium, compare what reputable dealers charge for that exact product, at that moment.
