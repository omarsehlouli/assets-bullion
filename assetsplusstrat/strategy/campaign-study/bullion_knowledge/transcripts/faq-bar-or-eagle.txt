Is it better to buy a one-ounce gold bar, or an American Gold Eagle?
There's no single answer, because preferences change.
Our owner has spent more than thirty years in precious metals, and has watched it happen.
Years ago, buyers favored government coins, especially the Canadian Gold Maple Leaf: twenty-four karat, point nine nine nine nine fine.
Then many moved to the American Gold Eagle. A little alloy makes it harder and more scratch-resistant, and it still holds a full troy ounce of gold.
Over roughly the past year, one-ounce bars have overtaken coins with our customers, along with smaller gram bars.
Eagles remain highly recognizable and liquid. The best choice depends on the premium, how much you're buying, and your preference.
