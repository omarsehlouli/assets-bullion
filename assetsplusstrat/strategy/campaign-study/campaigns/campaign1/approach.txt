Your First Piece

Audience: Adults considering their first physical gold or silver purchase.
Market: U.S.
Objective: Purchases of physical gold and silver bullion

Approach
Make the first purchase feel understandable. Show one silver coin and one small gold bar, invite comparison of weights and product details, and send high-intent traffic directly to the appropriate metal category. Lead with choice and clarity rather than financial urgency.

Hypothesis (not a result)
A tangible, approachable product pair may reduce first-purchase intimidation and earn qualified product exploration.

Transcript inspiration
first-piece, faq-bar-or-eagle

Execution
Google Search: separate ad groups by destination and intent. Meta: the same concept, primary text and product image. Purchases are the primary conversion; product views and carts are diagnostic. Validate purchase tracking before any live test.

Product and copy limits
Keep gold and silver search ad groups on matching destinations. No minimum starting price, returns promise, or claim that every buyer can afford a particular item.
