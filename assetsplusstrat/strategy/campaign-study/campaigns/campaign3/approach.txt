A Little Gold. A Lasting Gesture.

Audience: Adults shopping for a milestone or anniversary gift and considering a physical gold keepsake.
Market: U.S.
Objective: Purchases of physical gold and silver bullion

Approach
Use the emotional appeal of a tangible gift while keeping the product and purchase path specific. Show a small PAMP bar in a styled gifting scene and route the main ad to the matching 5 g product. Offer silver gifts through supporting links, without turning this into a sixth campaign.

Hypothesis (not a result)
The gifting scene may attract more attention and emotional interest, with purchase intent concentrated among people who already have an occasion in mind.

Transcript inspiration
meaningful-gift, traditions

Execution
Google Search: separate ad groups by destination and intent. Meta: the same concept, primary text and product image. Purchases are the primary conversion; product views and carts are diagnostic. Validate purchase tracking before any live test.

Product and copy limits
Box and ribbon are illustrative styling, not a promised inclusion. Use broad occasion-based creative; do not infer a viewer’s religion. Confirm stock and delivery timing before seasonal use.
