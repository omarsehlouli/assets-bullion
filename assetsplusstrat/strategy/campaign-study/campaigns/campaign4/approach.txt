Gold Has a New York Address

Audience: Adults in the New York metro area who prefer a reachable local dealer and an in-person purchase conversation.
Market: NYC local
Objective: Purchases of physical gold and silver bullion

Approach
Make the physical business the reason to choose this dealer. Pair the catalog products with a conceptual counter conversation, the verified Diamond District address, and a direct gold-shopping link. Add location and phone links as secondary actions; keep purchasing as the primary outcome.

Hypothesis (not a result)
A verifiable Manhattan location and a human conversation may improve trust and purchase intent among local buyers with authenticity concerns.

Transcript inspiration
new-york, faq-real-gold, real-or-fake

Execution
Google Search: separate ad groups by destination and intent. Meta: the same concept, primary text and product image. Purchases are the primary conversion; product views and carts are diagnostic. Validate purchase tracking before any live test.

Product and copy limits
Do not present the generated counter or hands as actual staff/store photography. Do not imply any simple home test proves authenticity. Confirm business hours before a visit.
