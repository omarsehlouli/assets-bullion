## What it is

An 81 s vertical ad for **Blurr**, a free Chrome extension that swaps personal details for placeholders
before a message reaches ChatGPT. There is no presenter: the piece is authored motion graphics over a calm,
measured male narrator, with a one-line word-highlight caption at the bottom.

## Why it works (interpretation)

It sells a product by making an abstract legal risk physical, then letting the product undo the exact
moment the viewer just watched go wrong. The arc:

1. **Rule** (0–11 s): a dated fact the viewer needs — new data law, data may not leave the country without consent.
2. **"Now picture this."** (12–21 s): one concrete, sympathetic scenario. A client, her passport, a manager
   in a hurry, one paste into ChatGPT. Nobody is malicious.
3. **Invisible consequence, made visible and timed** (22–30 s): globe, stopwatch 0.00→1.00 s, her record
   crosses the border, stamped WITHOUT CONSENT.
4. **Accountability turns to the viewer** (30–34 s): "Who answers for that? Not the manager. **You.**"
5. **The bill** (34–43 s): fine notice, amount counts up to 8,650,000 ₸.
6. **It's getting worse** (43–57 s): ×2 → ×4+ bar chart; a DRAFT law raises the fine; criminal liability.
7. **It's everywhere** (57–64 s): chat windows multiply 2×2 → 8×8 while a clock runs; "SENT · SENT · NO ALERTS".
8. **Direct question** (64–68 s): "Do you know what your team pasted into AI today?"
9. **Relief as replay** (69–74 s): "Don't worry." The *same* message, now with placeholders; the *same* globe
   shot, only placeholders cross the border.
10. **Brand + CTA** (74–81 s): B logo, "From the next message on, we've got you.", "Free for Chrome · blurr.info",
    Instagram outro.

The fear is specific and fair (one honest mistake), the stakes land on *you*, the evidence escalates from
one case to every office every day, and the product's answer is shown as the same scene going right.

## Recurring systems

- **The carried object.** The client record (name / IIN / phone) is the protagonist: CRM form → pasted text
  → tag on the globe → stamped card → paper-clipped to the fine notice → anonymised in the replay. The viewer
  follows one object through every scene; cuts change context around it.
- **Kicker + headline** (top-left): tiny mono caps kicker with a coloured dot ("• WHERE CHATGPT RUNS",
  "• HER DATA · ALMATY → ABROAD", "• THE FINE · WHO GETS IT"), then a white sans headline whose damaging
  half is set in red on a second line ("ChatGPT's servers / aren't in Kazakhstan.").
- **Word-highlight Caption** (bottom centre, ~78 % height): one short phrase, white bold sans; the current word
  sits on a purple rounded pill; upcoming words are dim grey. Purple = brand colour, used for every word.
- **Colour story**: teal = home / law / safe state; red = danger, loss, stamps; cream paper = official
  documents; purple = brand and the fix (caption pill, end card, replay line).
- **Stamps**: IN FORCE, WITHOUT CONSENT, DRAFT, NOT YET LAW, ANNOUNCED · NOT LAW YET — rotated, outlined,
  land with a small scale-down "thunk".
- **Count-ups**: stopwatch 0.00→1.00 s, money 0→8,650,000 ₸, multiplier ×2→×3→×4+, clock 09:53→16:34.
- **Strike-and-replace**: Manager struck through → line climbs to Director "YOUR NAME"; 2,000 MRP struck →
  5,000 MRP.
- **Transitions**: blur + zoom push-throughs between scenes; elements scale down and defocus as the next
  scene arrives; the camera dives into a detail (Director card → "You.").
- **Chrome**: brand handle top-right (Instagram glyph + @getblurr); source lines in tiny mono at the foot of
  data scenes; "fictional example" labels on documents; closing disclaimer "All people, companies and IDs in
  this film are fictional".

## Sound

a large impact on "You." (≈33.6 s, loudest moment of the film), and a rising sting into the logo (75–80 s).
Narrator: calm, low, unhurried; short sentences with real pauses.

## What to keep in an adaptation

The ten-beat argument, one carried object, the "Who …? Not X. You." turn, an escalating evidence passage on
paper documents, the multiplying-grid "every day" beat, the direct question, and above all the **replay**:
the product answers by re-running the same scene correctly. Keep brand colour reserved for brand/fix and
red for danger; keep the dark HUD restraint and the measured voice.
